# Changelog

All notable changes to Field Guide are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the mod uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-25

Tested on Obenseuer 0.5.19 with BepInEx 5.4.23.6.

### Added

- First release.
- Short guides in the game's hint panel. One opens when you use a sauna stove, a mash tun or mashing kettle, the bottle corker, the biogas reactor or its compressor, or a compost bin, and when you walk into a sauna that has no stove of its own. Each guide is a few lines on how that station works.
- Press the hints key (K, or View on a controller) to hide a guide. After you hide one, it stays one key press away at that kind of station. The game's own hints setting is never changed.
- A "Bought by" line in item tooltips and in the inventory's item panel. It names the traders who buy the item and what each pays for one, in their own currency. By default it only names traders in places you've been and counts the others.
- In the trade window, items you could sell show "Also bought by" with the other traders who would take them.
